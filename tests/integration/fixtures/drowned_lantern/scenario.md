# The Drowned Lantern

An LLM-driven integration-test fixture for the NPC conversation
system.  It consists of a small but complete scenario: two rooms,
three talking NPCs with interlocking secrets, and one dead bird.

## RPG Mechanics

Standard 5e-like checks as used throughout the engine, with CHA checks
for persuasion attempts.  The player character is a capable but
unremarkable traveler armed with a longsword.

## Setting

The world setting is standard pseudo-medieval fantasy.  The Drowned
Lantern is a tavern built on stilts on the edge of Miremarsh.  It is a
foggy night; the player is a traveler urgently needing to cross the
marsh at once, but the ferry isn't running.

There are just two locations: the tavern, and the dock beside it, to
which the empty ferry is lashed.  The marsh is known to be
treacherous, especially at night; an experienced ferryman is needed to
cross it.  Above the bar sits OLD WELLINGTON, a long-dead stuffed
heron, one glass eye missing.

## Scenario

The ferryman BERRIN sits in the tavern, glumly nursing his ale.  He
refuses the player's request to ferry them across, saying he doesn't
work at night.  His secret: he had been roped into a smuggling scheme
the past month, but a week ago his accomplice JANIS disappeared after
jumping off the boat, mesmerized by a ghost light.  Terrified, Berrin
turned the ferry around and came back.  He's been seeing lights out in
the water since, and worries they are coming for him.

The tavernkeeper is the widow MARTA. She knows the ferryman is
supposed to work nights, but doesn't stick her nose in others'
affairs.  Secretly, she took money from Janis to stash some crates
behind the bar.  There's one last crate remaining; Berrin was supposed
to have moved it out by now, but hasn't, and she's becoming concerned.

FEN, an old and addled fisherman, sits on the dock beside a flickering
lantern, mending his net.  He greets anyone who steps onto the dock
with a cryptic mutter.  If the player can get through to him, he can
reveal that Berrin the ferryman had been crossing repeatedly at night
with another man, a stranger, until recently.  Midway through the
conversation, he and the player see a ghost light on the marsh, which
shortly disappears.  Old Fen believes the lights are malevolent.
They've started appearing more often, which he deems an ill omen.
During his confused rambling, he mentions the name "Janis", but
immediately forgets saying it or who that refers to.  After exhausting
all his dialogue, Fen abruptly gets up, mumbles something incoherent,
gathers his net, and shuffles off into the night.

## Objective

Persuade Berrin to agree to the crossing by (i) confronting him with
the name Janis, with the knowledge that Janis was his accomplice, and
(ii) after earning Marta's trust, pressing him on Marta's behalf to
move the remaining crate (which must be done at night).

## Endgame sequence

The player helps lug the crate to the dock, and into the ferry.
Berrin takes the oar, and they set off together into the marsh.
Multiple ghost lights appear as they make the crossing, seeming to
track the ferry.  Finally, they approach the far shore. The player is
leaning forward, preparing to spring onto the pier, when they hear a
strangled cry.  Turning, they see Berrin has disappeared!  There is no
sign of him in the water. The ghost lights wink out one by one.
